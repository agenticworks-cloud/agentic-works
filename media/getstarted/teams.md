The **Teams** view in the activity bar lists your chats. Open one in a tab to read and reply.

**Sign In to Teams** opens Microsoft's sign-in page in your browser; this step waits until you are done. Sign in with
your work account, as you do for Teams on the web.

- The first time, Microsoft asks to allow **chirp**, the app Teams signs in with. If it says **Need admin approval**,
  ask your IT team to approve it. Until then Teams stays signed out, and this step says so; the rest of Agentic Works works.
- If your company has its own approved app, use **Teams: Use a Company Microsoft App…** with the two IDs from IT.
- **Let's keep your account secure** is Microsoft's own prompt: **Not now** carries on.

If one of your company's tools keeps the Teams sign-in, this step shows that tool's row instead, and signs in there.

Personal Microsoft accounts aren't supported. This step is ticked once Teams is signed in.
