# Connect Jira and Confluence

1. **Jira: Site URL**: your Atlassian site, for example `https://yourcompany.atlassian.net`.
2. **Jira: Sign In**: signs in to that site.

The Jira view in the activity bar then lists your tickets; **Jira: Open Ticket** and **Jira: Open Confluence Page**
open one by key or link.
